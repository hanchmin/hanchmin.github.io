I am a Tenure-track Associate Professor at the [Institute of Natural Sciences (INS)](https://ins.sjtu.edu.cn/) and the [School of Mathematics (SMS)](https://math.sjtu.edu.cn), [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/). My research centers around building mathematical principles that facilitate the interplay between machine learning and dynamical systems. Recently, I am mainly interested in analyzing gradient-based optimization algorithms on overparametrized neural networks from a dynamical system perspective.

<!-- MORE -->

Prior to joining SJTU, I was a Postdoc Researcher at the [Center for Innovation in Data Engineering and Science (IDEAS)](https://ideas.seas.upenn.edu/), University of Pennsylvania, advised by [Prof. René Vidal](http://vision.jhu.edu/rvidal.html) from 2023 to 2025. I received my Ph.D. degree from Johns Hopkins University, where I was fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Enrique Mallada](http://mallada.ece.jhu.edu) and [Prof. René Vidal](http://vision.jhu.edu/rvidal.html). Before Hopkins, I received a Master’s degree in Systems Engineering from the University of Pennsylvania and a Bachelor’s degree in Automation from Tongji University, Shanghai.
